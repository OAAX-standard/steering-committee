## Agenda

Theme: get OAAX 2.0 released.

1. Review the next steps from 2026-09-07 (status below).
2. **Release readiness.** Merge the 2.0 branches to `main`, repo by repo.
   - Which chips ship in 2.0 (roadmap lists CPU, Intel, NVIDIA, Hailo, DEEPX, MemryX, SiMa)? Anything without a 2.0 branch by the cut-off is deferred.
   - Release date, cut-off for merging, and the release policy (versioning, branches, support window).
3. **Prebuilt binaries.** Publish runtimes and toolchains for 2.0 (`runtimes/2.0.0/` on the artifact store currently returns 403). Where, who, and the layout `deploy-ultralytics fetch` expects.

6. **Excel sheet.** Convert the remaining tasks into issues with owners ([#5](https://github.com/OAAX-standard/steering-committee/issues/5)).
7. Roadmap update to reflect the decisions above.
